## LinkedIn version

**Banking Credit Risk & Decision Process Analysis** (personal portfolio project)

A Business Analyst case study simulating a credit process efficiency investigation
for a fictional bank, built end to end: business framing and stakeholder analysis,
a synthetic 20,000-application dataset with deliberately realistic data quality
issues, cleaning and validation against explicit business rules, KPI and decision
analysis in Python cross-checked against independently written SQL queries, an
As-Is/To-Be process redesign, 10 MoSCoW-prioritized functional requirements with
user stories and acceptance criteria, and a Power BI dashboard specification.

Key finding: manual review, while only 15.5% of the portfolio, is associated with
a 4.5x increase in median processing time and a 14% customer withdrawal rate -
evidence that the process's biggest friction point is not the credit decision
itself, but the wait around it. Recommendations focus on reducing unnecessary
manual review and giving customers visibility during the wait, without automating
away the human decision where the data shows it still changes outcomes.

Tools: Python (pandas, numpy), SQL (SQLite), Power BI (specification), Jupyter,
Git/GitHub.

This is the second of two related, complementary case studies at the same
fictional bank, Chevron Constantine Banking - the first covers digital customer
journey and abandonment; this one covers credit risk and decision process
efficiency.
